# IME tests in ACT4: progress as of 2026-11-21

Details, pins and commands are in [HANDOFF.md](HANDOFF.md). The step log is [udb-0.9.0.md](udb-0.9.0.md).
Bugs and issue drafts are in [../issues/INDEX.md](../issues/INDEX.md).

## Done

The IME tests now exist in native ACT4 form on `cgomez/ime-udb-0.9.0`: 15 commits on `act4` @ `7569d06d6`.
Nothing is pushed.

- **Definitions.** riscv-unified-db `ime/fp-mx` defines all 65 IME extensions and the FP disclosure parameters.
  The ACT-local overlay is removed.
- **Reference model.** The Sail fork `cleanup/ime-0.9.0` @ `0ebfc810` computes the expected values. It follows spec
  `58810918`, like UDB and the tests.
- **Suites.** Each suite expands per SEW, 8 to 64:
  - `Zvvmm`: 13 integer MACC rows and 4 tile-MACC rows.
  - `Zvvfmm`: 16 FP MACC rows (FP16, BF16, FP32, FP64, the widening forms, OFP8 and OFP4 inputs) and 28 microscaled
    rows, 14 of them with integer inputs.
  - `Zvvmtls` and `Zvvmttls`: tile loads and stores, with cross-mode pairs.
  - `ExceptionsZvvm`: illegal-instruction and `vstart` cases, including the microscaled cases.
- **FP data.** Results do not depend on the disclosed FP profile (G, psm, rnd), except in gated `cp_fp_rnd`
  testcases. Those run only on a profile that discloses rnd=frm and psm=0.
- **Coverage.** covergroupgen generates the IME covergroups from the testplans. vm=0 instructions are sampled.
  Each FP covergroup samples only its own formats (`altfmt`, `altfmt_A`, `altfmt_B`).
- **Results.**
  - `sail-ime-rv64` (Sail as DUT, VLEN 1024): 93/93 files pass.
  - `qemu-ime-rv64` (SDK QEMU, VLEN 256): the integer and tile suites pass. The FP and exception failures are QEMU
    deviations from the spec.
  - UDB C++ ISS cross-check: 9,716 of 9,858 IME results equal Sail. The other 142 have different inputs, because the
    ISS does not implement the V instructions used for setup. No IME result mismatch was found.
- **Checks.** pyslang compiled the covergroups that each step changed, with 0 errors. No Questa or VCS is
  available, so the bins have not been measured.
